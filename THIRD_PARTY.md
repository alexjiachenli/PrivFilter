PrivFilter builds on the [FLDD research code](https://github.com/alexjiachenli/fldd).
Source revisions and file hashes are recorded in `SOURCE_MANIFEST.json` and
`reproduction/manifest.json`.

[FilterFL](https://github.com/easywood0204/FilterFL) by Yang et al. (CCS 2025).
These portions remain under the FilterFL authors' terms. Baseline modules retain
their method citations and implementation scope.

`2ff57b2b4d718f9665f4b2ac8245b0bcd7e65165`, with the compatibility patch stored
in `patches/`. Its source includes CrypTen copyright and MIT-license notices.

Upstream copyright and license notices are preserved.
